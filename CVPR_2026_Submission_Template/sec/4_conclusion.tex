\section{Conclusion}
We present a compact and reproducible language-driven grasp detection system
based on LGDM and LSAR.  The LSAR residual conditioning improves spatial
grounding and consistently increases correct-grasp rate over the repaired
baseline on a 10{,}000-scene Grasp-Anything++ subset.  Results are limited to
a subset, 15 training epochs, and 10-step sampling; full-scale training and
real-robot validation are left as future work.
